Connecting to After Effects…
Choose a target, then align or distribute selected layers. Key layer mode automatically uses the first selected layer and keeps it fixed; select the key first, then add the movers. Spacing +/− changes the value by 1; Shift-click moves to the next multiple of 10 in that direction. Nulls align and distribute by their anchor points; their children follow normally. Use Options > Animation > Offset all keys to shift an animated controller as a whole. Options > Text > Balanced uses cap height and baselines to keep descenders from shifting vertical balance. Choose Visible edges for glyph edges including overshoot. Unsupported typography uses visible bounds with a status note. Paragraph box keeps its layout box. Enabled Position expressions keep their animation and receive a continuous offset across time, regardless of the Animation option. Each command is one Undo step. Bounds are rectangles; masks, effects and per-character text transforms are not measured. Safe areas are fixed at 90% and 80%. All-key offsets are measured at the current frame. Scroll anywhere in this panel to reach all options.
